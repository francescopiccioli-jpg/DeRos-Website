Allergens

Numbers in brackets indicate allergens.

Pursuant to Annex II of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 on the provision of food information to consumers, we list the 14 allergens that may cause intolerances and allergies in sensitive individuals.

  1. 1Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt, Kamut and their hybridised strains) and products thereof.
  2. 2Crustaceans and products thereof.
  3. 3Eggs and products thereof.
  4. 4Fish and products thereof.
  5. 5Milk and products thereof.
  6. 6Peanuts and products thereof.
  7. 7Soybeans and products thereof.
  8. 8Tree nuts (almonds, hazelnuts, walnuts, pistachios) and products thereof.
  9. 9Celery and products thereof.
  10. 10Mustard and products thereof.
  11. 11Sesame seeds and products thereof.
  12. 12Sulphur dioxide and sulphites at concentrations above 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/litre, expressed as total SO₂.
  13. 13Lupin and products thereof.
  14. 14Molluscs and products thereof.

When ordering, please tell our staff about any intolerances or allergies, so that every necessary precaution can be taken in preparing your dishes.
